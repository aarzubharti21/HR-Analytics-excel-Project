# HR Analytics Excel Project

## Overview

I am excited to present the HR Analytics Dashboard, a comprehensive Excel analytics project developed to transform raw employee data into meaningful business intelligence. This project provides an interactive reporting solution that enables HR professionals and business leaders to monitor workforce performance, employee attrition, job satisfaction, departmental trends, compensation patterns, and overall organizational health. The dashboard delivers actionable insights that support strategic workforce planning, employee retention, and data-driven human resource management.

## Problem Statement

The primary objective of this project was to develop a comprehensive HR monitoring solution capable of analyzing employee demographics, workforce distribution, salary structure, education background, job roles, and attrition patterns. The available HR dataset contained employee information across multiple business dimensions, making it difficult to identify the factors influencing employee turnover, workforce performance, and organizational stability. The project required the development of an interactive Excel dashboard that enables HR teams to dynamically analyze workforce data and make informed talent management decisions.

## Analysis Done

The file in which the analysis is done is HR Analytics Dashboard.xlsx

A structured data preparation and dashboard development approach was implemented to ensure accurate reporting, meaningful insights, and interactive business intelligence. The analytical workflow included:

- **Data Cleaning**: Processed the HR dataset by handling missing values, removing duplicate employee records, validating data types, and standardizing employee information for consistent analysis.
  
- **Data Modeling**: Organized employee records into structured Excel tables and established relationships between workforce attributes to support efficient reporting using Pivot Tables and Pivot Charts.
  
- **KPI Development**: Developed key HR performance indicators including **Total Employees**, **Attrition Count**, **Attrition Rate**, **Active Employees**, **Average Age**, **Average Salary**, **Average Years at Company**, and **Job Satisfaction Score**.
  
- **Employee Demographic Analysis**: Examined workforce distribution based on age, gender, education, marital status, department, and business travel to understand employee composition across the organization.
  
- **Attrition Analysis**: Analyzed employee turnover across departments, job roles, education levels, salary bands, overtime status, and years of experience to identify major drivers of attrition.
  
- **Performance & Compensation Analysis**: Evaluated salary distribution, monthly income, job satisfaction, work-life balance, and employee performance metrics to understand factors influencing employee engagement.
  
- **Interactive Dashboard Development**: Built a dynamic Excel dashboard using Pivot Tables, Pivot Charts, KPIs, and Slicers, allowing users to perform detailed workforce analysis through interactive filtering.

## Dashboard

The final deliverable consists of a highly interactive Excel dashboard that provides a comprehensive overview of workforce performance and employee analytics through dynamic visualizations and cross-filtering capabilities.

## Dashboard Features

- **HR Performance Overview**: Displays key performance indicators including Total Employees, Attrition Count, Attrition Rate, Active Employees, Average Age, Average Salary, and Average Years at Company, providing a complete snapshot of organizational health.
  
- **Department-wise Attrition Analysis**: Compares employee attrition across departments to identify business units experiencing the highest workforce turnover.
  
- **Job Role Analysis**: Evaluates employee distribution and attrition across different job roles, helping HR identify positions with higher employee resignation rates.
  
- **Age Group Analysis**: Analyzes workforce demographics by age categories to understand employee distribution and retention trends.
  
- **Salary Band Analysis**: Examines employee attrition across different salary ranges to identify whether compensation influences employee retention.
  
- **Education Field Analysis**: Evaluates workforce composition based on educational backgrounds, helping organizations understand hiring and retention trends.
  
- **Gender Distribution Analysis**: Compares workforce participation and attrition between male and female employees to support diversity and inclusion initiatives.
  
- **Job Satisfaction & Work-Life Balance Analysis**: Assesses employee satisfaction levels and work-life balance ratings to identify factors contributing to employee engagement and retention.
  
- **Interactive Filters**: Enables users to dynamically explore workforce data using slicers for Department, Job Role, Education, Gender, Age Group, Business Travel, Marital Status, and other employee attributes.

## Recommendations

Based on the insights generated from the dashboard, the following strategic recommendations are highly recommended to improve workforce performance and employee retention:

- **Reduce Employee Attrition**: Focus retention strategies on departments and job roles experiencing the highest employee turnover through targeted engagement programs.
  
- **Enhance Employee Satisfaction**: Regularly monitor job satisfaction and work-life balance indicators while implementing initiatives that improve employee well-being and motivation.
  
- **Review Compensation Policies**: Evaluate salary structures for roles with higher attrition to ensure compensation remains competitive within the industry.
  
- **Strengthen Career Development**: Provide structured learning opportunities, career progression plans, mentoring programs, and internal promotions to improve employee retention.
  
- **Improve Workforce Planning**: Utilize employee demographic and performance insights to optimize hiring strategies, succession planning, and workforce allocation.
  
- **Promote Employee Engagement**: Conduct regular employee feedback surveys, recognition programs, and leadership development initiatives to increase engagement and organizational commitment.
  
- **Leverage HR Analytics for Decision-Making**: Continuously monitor workforce KPIs through the dashboard to identify emerging HR trends, reduce attrition, improve productivity, and support long-term organizational growth.
